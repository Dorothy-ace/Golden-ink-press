FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I open the book categories? Click The Vault (☰) on the top left of the home page to slide open all shelves.
  • How do I search for a specific story or character? Type any tag (e.g., rivals-to-lovers), character name (e.g., Sarawat), work title, or bookmarked into the search box and press Search.
  • Can I download books to read offline? Yes—open any book card and click Download Book Copy to save a formatted reading file.
